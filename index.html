Rules

How turns work
Fen acts first each round, then you choose a move. After your move, Effort regenerates, R/C build-up may adjust, and timed effects (like Confident) count down before Fen's next turn.

Resources
HP — your health and Fen's. The battle ends the instant either side's HP reaches 0.
Effort — spent on Facts and Feelings. Regenerates +2 per round under standard conditions, capped at your max.
👄 (Rhetoric build-up) — builds +1 each time Rhetoric is used under standard conditions, capped at 10. Facts spend some of it to cast.
🧠 (Consideration build-up) — builds +1 each time Consideration is used under standard conditions, capped at 10. Feelings spend some of it to cast.
🛡️ Defence — a flat reduction applied to incoming damage before any other modifier.
😤 Confident — a temporary status that changes how certain moves land.

Goal
Reduce your opponent's HP to 0.
