Rules

How turns work
The player takes turns debating their opponent. The player goes first. Once the debate is underway, the player can interpret vaguely what their opponent might do next. The debate ends when one participant is defeated.

Resources
HP — Health. The battle ends when this drops to zero.
Effort — spent on Facts and Feelings. Regenerates +2 per round under standard conditions, capped at your max.
💥 Exposed — experiences double damage from Facts for 1 turn.
💥 Primed — its owner's own next attack deals a flat bonus.

Goal
Reduce your opponent's HP to 0.
