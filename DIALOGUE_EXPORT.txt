RAT LAND — FULL DIALOGUE EXPORT
================================
Extracted from NPC_DIALOGUE.md (every NPC) and js/npc.js (in-game line
data, used to confirm exact wording/order) plus Fen Wicket's full battle
dialogue from js/combat.js (the implementation of the moveset described
in COMBAT_DESIGN.md). Locations are the nearest named landmark on the
overworld map (js/map.js RatLand.LOCATIONS) to each character's map
position, given as approximate col/row grid coordinates.

Where a character's written material includes a two-rat "Exchange"
passage from NPC_DIALOGUE.md, that character's own lines from the
exchange are included in their numbered list, tagged with which other
character they're exchanging with, so no line is lost even though it
only makes full sense in context.

Character location: Overworld — near Church of the Rat God (col 4, row 6)

Character topics of conversation: Pro-separation, patriotic duty to Rat Land's twenty years of independence, pride in the borough and its "drainpipe."
