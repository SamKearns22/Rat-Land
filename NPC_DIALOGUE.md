# NPC Dialogue — Rat Land

Reference doc for future sessions: dialogue and personality notes for
Rat Land's NPCs, written for the 20th anniversary setting described in
`WORLD.md`. Twenty rats plus one mouse. Each entry includes a short
**Stance** line (personality, class, tone) meant to guide later work —
sprite design, placement, whatever needs to match "who this character
actually is" rather than a generic category.

Group 1 satirizes the Rat Land / Mouse Land separation debate. The
spread is deliberate: earnest and rude, posh and rough, on both sides
of the argument, plus apathy, conspiracy, and nostalgia. No side is
written to be objectively correct — the joke is always on the rat, not
the position. Group 2 is politics-free: weather, football, prices,
and wistfulness for the surface.

---

## Group 1 — Political Rats

### Colonel Bartholomew Nutkin
**Stance:** Pro-separation / patriotic. Posh, ex-Sewer Regiment, earnest — sincere, not rude.

- "We didn't spend twenty years excavating this borough for it to become a thoroughfare, thank you very much."
- "I've nothing against the mice personally. Splendid whiskers, some of them. It's a matter of principle."
- "A rat who won't defend his own drainpipe is no rat at all. Chin up. Tail straight."

### Gristle
**Stance:** Pro-separation, economic-and-cultural resentment mixed together. Rough dockside rat, blunt, genuinely rude.

- "Shut the grate, that's what I say. Simple as."
- "Every time I try to complain about somethin', someone tells me I'm 'not looking at the bigger picture.' I am. It's smaller than it used to be, is what."
- "Nothin' personal. Well. It's a bit personal."

### Marguerite Thistlewood-Vole
**Stance:** Pro-open-borders. Posh, sanctimonious — rude via contempt for anyone she considers small-minded.

- "One does wonder how some rats sleep at night, frankly, with views like that rattling round such small burrows."
- "We ought to be a touch more hospitable, don't you think? It costs nothing to be gracious. Well — it costs a little. But one absorbs it."
- "I had the most marvellous mouse caterer for my anniversary do. Divine little vol-au-vents. It's really opened my eyes."

**Exchange — Marguerite and Gristle, in passing:**
> **Marguerite:** "One does wonder how some rats sleep at night, with views like that."
> **Gristle:** "Like a log, thanks. Doesn't cost me nothin' to keep my own tunnel my own tunnel."
> **Marguerite:** "How refreshingly small of you."
> **Gristle:** "Takes one to know one, your ladyship."

### Pip
**Stance:** Pro-open-borders. Young, working-class, earnest — warm rather than preachy.

- "Me mum always said a full sewer's a happy sewer. More whiskers, more warmth, that's what I reckon."
- "Dunno about all the fuss, honestly. A rat's a rat. A mouse works just as hard for a crust as the rest of us."

### The Drain-Watcher
**Stance:** Cynical / apathetic. Unnamed by choice — mocks both sides equally, invested in neither.

- "Separation, unification, whatever — none of it's fixed my leaky pipe, has it."
- "They're all up there shouting past each other. I just want the shouting to happen somewhere drier."
- "Vote for whoever you like. I'm voting for a nap."

### Twitchy Nostrum
**Stance:** Conspiratorial. Convinced the whole debate is a distraction engineered by someone, somewhere, for reasons unclear even to him.

- "You don't hear much about who's really running the cheese supply, do you? Funny, that."
- "The whole separation debate? Distraction. Someone up top wants us squabbling so we don't look at the U-bend."
- "I'm not saying it's the Overground rats. I'm saying nobody's ruled it out."

### Nan Gribble
**Stance:** Nostalgic for "the old sewers." Elderly, rambling, doesn't fully engage with modern politics — remembers a simpler (murkier) time.

- "In my day the water ran browner and nobody made a fuss about it."
- "We didn't have 'sides.' We had one good tunnel and you were grateful for it."
- "Mouse Land, Rat Land — in my day it was just 'the wet bit' and 'the wetter bit.'"

### Bramwell
**Stance:** Separation-sympathetic but conflicted. Carries the unresolved personal thread — bravado that cracks, then gets quickly covered back up.

- "Best thing we ever did, drawing that line. Keeps things tidy."
- "...My sister crossed over, you know. Mouse Land side. Eleven years this spring. Not that I'm counting."
- "Anyway. Tidy. That's the main thing."

### Ferdy Cobb
**Stance:** Pro-separation for economic reasons, not cultural ones. Working-class, union rat, earnest — distinguishes himself from Nutkin's patriotism.

- "It's not about mice, personally, it's about wages. Cheese-hauling work's been split three ways since the crossing opened."
- "Man's got to feed his kits. Nothin' fancy about it, whatever the Colonel dresses it up as."

### The Wavering Rat
**Stance:** Confused moderate. Unnamed — satire of the rat who will not, under any circumstances, commit to a position.

- "I go back and forth, don't I. Monday I'm all for the wall. Tuesday I feel dreadful about it."
- "Honestly I just nod along with whoever's talking. Saves an argument."

## Group 2 — Ordinary Rats

### Doreen
**Stance:** Runs the counter at Rat Café. Warm, unbothered, generous with crusts — treats Kevin the mouse like any other regular.

- "Damp again today. Mind you, it's always damp. I mean proper wet-through damp."
- "Saved you the good crusts, love — oh, not you, I meant the fella behind you. But you can have one too."

### Sooty and Chalky
**Stance:** Football rivals — Outfall Rovers vs. Big Sump United. Light, needling, entirely apolitical.

**Exchange:**
> **Sooty:** "Outfall Rovers put four past Sump on Sunday. Four!"
> **Chalky:** "Against ten rats and a ref who couldn't see past his own whiskers."
> **Sooty:** "Excuses are the only thing Sump's any good at collecting these days."

### Old Fenwick
**Stance:** Cheese-price grumbler. Nostalgic in a much smaller, more edible way than Nan Gribble.

- "Cheddar's up again. At this rate we'll be back to gnawing candle stubs."
- "Used to get a whole rind for tuppence. Now they charge you to look at it."

### The Scrap Grumbler
**Stance:** Unnamed. Bottle-cap economy specialist, perpetually aggrieved by the market.

- "Bottle caps are through the floor. Through the floor! And I've got a whole hoard of the things."
- "Man down the shopping district offered me half what he did last spring. Half!"

### The Countryside Dreamer
**Stance:** Unnamed. Wistful about the surface — real grass, real soil, someday.

- "Sometimes I dream about grass. Actual grass. Not the mossy sort — the proper green kind, up top."
- "They say there's a garden two streets over with real soil. I'm saving my scraps for the trip."

### Two Rats on the Weather
**Stance:** Unnamed pair. The most British possible exchange.

**Exchange:**
> **Rat A:** "Damp again."
> **Rat B:** "Extra damp, actually. I checked the drip gauge this morning."
> **Rat A:** "You have a drip gauge?"
> **Rat B:** "Everyone should have a drip gauge."

### The Queue Complainer
**Stance:** Unnamed. Perpetually behind someone slow at Rat Café.

- "Twenty minutes for a coffee-rind and a nibble. Twenty minutes!"
- "Whole town's queuing for something these days. Queuing's basically our national pastime."

### The Allotment Bragger
**Stance:** Unnamed. Fiercely proud of a patch of moss.

- "My moss patch took Best in Show at the tunnel fair. Third year running."
- "Secret's compost. Don't let anyone tell you otherwise."

### The Parade Gossip
**Stance:** Unnamed. Excited about the 20th anniversary bunting, nothing more.

- "Twenty years! Can you believe it. They're doing bunting all down Main Street."
- "I heard the Mayor's ordering a whole new sash for the occasion. Velvet, apparently."

### The Bin Day Grumbler
**Stance:** Unnamed. Aggrieved by an unreliable scrap collection schedule.

- "Scrap collection's moved again. Third time this year. Nobody tells you anything round here."
- "I had a perfectly good bottle cap out for collection Tuesday. Still there Friday. Tragic."

## Outsider — Kevin (mouse)

**Stance:** Unassuming, weary-but-good-humored. Born on Culvert Street, in Rat Land, and treated as a foreigner anyway. Two alternate lines, depending on who's nearby.

- *(Near the political rats)* "Colonel Nutkin tips his hat to me every morning — right before asking when I'm 'heading back.' Lovely manners. Terrible math. I was born on Culvert Street."
- *(Near the ordinary rats)* "Doreen saves me the good crusts down the Café. Reckons a mouse who queues properly is alright by her. That's about the whole of it, really."
