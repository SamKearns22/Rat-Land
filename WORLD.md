# Rat Land

Rat Land is a sewer town built by and for rats, tucked in the pipes and
tunnels beneath a much larger human city. This year, Rat Land is celebrating
its 20th anniversary of founding — bunting, parades, and civic pride are
everywhere. But underneath the patriotic surface, the town is fraying:

- **Rising tension with the immigrant mouse population.** Mice have been
  moving into Rat Land in greater numbers, drawn by the town's relative
  prosperity. Many rats resent them as competitors for food, scraps, and
  housing; mice largely live in the poorer, more cramped corners of town
  and are shut out of political power.
- **Wealth inequality.** A small class of well-connected rats controls the
  best real estate (the driest pipes, the choicest garbage routes) while
  most residents scrape by. The gap is visible in the town's two flagship
  pubs alone.
- **Political instability.** Rat Town Hall is riddled with factionalism,
  patronage, and quiet corruption behind the anniversary celebrations.
- **General social malaise.** Despite the flags and festivities, there's a
  pervasive undercurrent of anxiety, resentment, and distrust simmering
  beneath Rat Land's cheerful public face.

## Locations

- **Rat Café** — A cramped, steam-fogged coffee spot where gossip about
  town politics spreads faster than the smell of burnt beans.
- **Church of the Rat God** — The town's spiritual center, where old rites
  and modern anxieties mix under a shrine of gnawed bones and candle wax.
- **The Sewer River (with a few bridges)** — The murky waterway running
  through town, crossed by a handful of rickety bridges that double as
  informal borders between neighborhoods.
- **Rat Beach** — A muddy stretch along the Sewer River where rats sunbathe
  under a distant grate of daylight, pretending the water is cleaner than
  it is.
- **Rat Park** — The town's green space (such as it is), host to the
  anniversary parade route and increasingly tense public gatherings.
- **Rat Town Hall** — Seat of Rat Land's government, where the mayor and
  council navigate patronage, scandal, and the optics of a milestone
  anniversary.
- **Rat Shopping District** — The commercial strip of scavenged-goods
  stalls and shops, where the wealth gap between merchants is hard to miss.
- **Rat School** — Where young rats (and a handful of mouse students) are
  educated, and where the town's tensions first show up among the kids.
- **Rat Gymnasium** — A multipurpose hall used for sports, assemblies, and
  the occasional heated town meeting that spills over from Town Hall.
- **The Gilded Rat (fancy pub)** — An upscale watering hole for Rat Land's
  elite, dripping with polished bottle-cap chandeliers and old money.
- **The Rusty Pipe (working-class pub)** — A no-frills bar where most of
  the town actually drinks, and where anti-mouse sentiment is loudest.
- **Mousque** — A small mouse place of worship at the edge of town,
  tucked in the bottom-right corner of the map. Not yet a working
  interior, same as most of the list above.
